Bootcamp Breakdown

Learn, Compete, Respond.

PromptQuest blended deep adversarial AI theory with immediate, competitive hands-on execution across three distinct phases.

Session 1 · 1 Hour

Prompt Hacking Techniques & Defenses

A comprehensive architectural walkthrough covering both sides of LLM exploitation: how attackers craft adversarial prompts to bypass safeguards, and the multi-layer defenses that stop them.

  • Fundamental attack vectors: Instruction injection, jailbreaks, prompt leaking
  • Context manipulation: Context ignoring, distractor commands, context switching
  • Evasion tricks: Token smuggling, typos, obfuscation & fill-in-the-blank
  • Defensive controls: XML tagging, random sequence enclosure, few-shot boundaries
Session 2 · 2 Hours

Live Prompt Red-Teaming Hackathon

Participants were challenged to put their knowledge to the test in a live, timed arena. Engineering teams competed in real time to outsmart and red-team production-like AI systems.

  • Interactive challenge pods with live scoring dashboards
  • Bypassing simulated LLM guardrails under restricted conditions
  • Extracting hidden system prompts and bypassing content policies
  • Peer review of successful attack vectors and defensive countermeasures
Session 3 · 1.5 Hours

Simulated AI Crisis Tabletop Drill

An executive and engineering crisis simulation based on real-world incident escalation. Teams navigated simulated prompt injection crises impacting corporate systems.

  • 3 timed crisis injects that progressively escalated severity
  • Cross-functional coordination between engineering, product, and ops
  • Real-time incident response, patch deployment, and mitigation
  • Guided post-mortem walkthroughs of enterprise-grade response drills
