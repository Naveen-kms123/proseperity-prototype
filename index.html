The gap we close

AI is everywhere. People who can build it responsibly are not.

Organizations are adopting Generative AI, RAG, Agents, and intelligent workflows at speed. But there is a consistent gap between awareness and the ability to build governed, cost-efficient, production-grade AI.

01

Demos don't become products

Most AI experimentation stays in notebooks. Teams know the concept but struggle to build a production API, add validation guardrails, or trace operational cost.

02

AI runs blind and expensive

Without observability and cost governance, applications waste tokens, leak PII into prompts, and output unstructured responses that break downstream systems.

03

Theory doesn't transfer to code

Courses teach concepts. The gap is in taking understanding of LLMs, RAG, and agents and writing governed code that can be deployed, tested, and audited.

How we train

Instructor-led. Hands-on labs. Real AI systems, built by you.

Our bootcamps are structured so that every concept is immediately applied in code. By the time you leave, you have a working AI backend — not slides.

1

Instructor-led sessions with live demos

Every concept is walked through live in code with the instructor. You see how it works, then immediately build it in your own environment.

2

Guided hands-on labs

Labs are structured with step-by-step guidance. You are never left stuck on a blank screen; instructors guide you through real errors and trace outputs.

3

Team capstone build

The final phase is a team-based capstone where you apply everything — RAG, guardrails, agents, and observability — to a real problem. You present and keep the code.
